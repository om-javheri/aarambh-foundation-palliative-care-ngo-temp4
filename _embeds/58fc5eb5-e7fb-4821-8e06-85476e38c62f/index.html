The Beginning of Aarambh

Our Story

Aarambh Foundation was not started as an idea—it was born out of a deeply personal and life-changing experience. In 2013-14, Pradeep Kakade witnessed a devastating reality when a close relative with advanced cancer was sent home with no further curative treatment possible.

"The patient was lying on a mat inside a small hut, almost abandoned. His wound had worsened severely. What troubled me even more was that due to fear and lack of awareness, his own family did not know how to care for him."

Determined to find a way to help, he researched and discovered Palliative Care. Despite not having a medical background, his persistence over two years led to formal training. In 2017, he left his stable job to dedicate himself completely to this mission.

❤ Sometimes, one painful moment can give birth to a lifetime of purpose. Aarambh is that purpose.

Our Services

What We Do

Home-Based Palliative Care

Delivering medical and emotional support directly to the patient's home, ensuring dignity in familiar surroundings.

Support Groups

Creating safe spaces for patients and caregivers to share experiences naturally, reducing isolation.

Counselling Services

Providing psychological and emotional counselling for families navigating the end-of-life journey.

Awareness Campaigns

Educating communities about cancer prevention, palliative care, and breaking stigmas.

Comfort-Centered Care with Dignity

Ensuring peaceful, pain-free passing with absolute dignity and clinical comfort management.
